About Us

Modern Computers E-Sevai Center is a trusted service center dedicated to providing fast, reliable, and convenient digital services to the public. We assist citizens with a wide range of Tamil Nadu Government e-Sevai services, CSC services, and computer-related services under one roof.

Our goal is to make government and online services easily accessible while ensuring transparency, accuracy, and customer satisfaction. We are committed to providing timely assistance for certificates, registrations, online applications, document printing, scanning, lamination, and other digital services.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver reliable, affordable, and citizen-friendly digital services that simplify access to government schemes and online facilities. We strive to provide fast, accurate, and transparent services while ensuring convenience, trust, and complete customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the most trusted and customer-focused e-Sevai center by delivering efficient, transparent, and high-quality services to every citizen. We aspire to make digital and government services easily accessible, ensuring convenience, reliability, and satisfaction for every customer while contributing to a digitally empowered and well-connected community.
